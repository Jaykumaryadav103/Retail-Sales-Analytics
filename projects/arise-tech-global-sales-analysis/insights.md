# A few things in the sample data

These numbers come from made-up practice orders. They are not results from a real store.

- There are 300 orders: 254 delivered, 27 returned, and 19 cancelled.
- Sales from delivered orders total about ₹848,895 after discounts.
- Fashion has the highest sales of the four categories in this sample.
- Chennai has the highest sales among the six cities in this sample.

## What I would check next

- Which products are returned most often?
- Do orders with bigger discounts get returned more often?
- Are sales changes month to month caused by more orders or larger orders?

These are starting points for further analysis. The sample is small, so I would check more data before making a business recommendation.
